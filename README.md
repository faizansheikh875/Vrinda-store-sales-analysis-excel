📊 Vrinda Store Sales Analysis Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data of Vrinda Store to identify customer behavior patterns, sales trends, and high-performing regions and sales channels.

An interactive Excel dashboard was created to visualize insights and support business decision-making.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze month-wise sales performance

Identify gender-based customer contribution

Segment customers by age group

Evaluate state-wise sales performance

Compare sales across different online channels

🛠 Tools & Techniques Used

Microsoft Excel

Data Cleaning & Formatting

Pivot Tables

Pivot Charts

Slicers (Month, Gender, Channel)

Interactive Dashboard Design

📊 Dashboard Features

3 Pie Charts

2 Bar Charts

Age Group Segmentation (Teenager, Adult, Senior)

State-wise Sales Analysis

Channel-wise Sales Comparison

Dynamic filtering using slicers

🔎 Key Insights

Women customers contribute approximately 65% of total sales.

The 30–49 years age group generates around 50% of total revenue.

Top-performing states are Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh, contributing nearly 35% of total sales.

Major sales channels include Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra, accounting for about 80% of overall sales.

🎯 Business Recommendations

Focus marketing campaigns on women aged 30–49 years.

Strengthen promotions in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.

Offer seasonal discounts and personalized coupons.

Increase advertisement spending on Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra platforms.

By aligning strategy with high-performing customer segments and channels, Vrinda Store can significantly improve revenue growth.
